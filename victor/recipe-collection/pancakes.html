About This Recipe

Pancakes are soft, round cakes prepared on a hot pan. They are popular for breakfast and can be served with honey, fruit, syrup, or other toppings.

Recipe Information

  • Preparation time: 10 minutes
  • Cooking time: 15 minutes
  • Total time: 25 minutes
  • Servings: 4 people
  • Difficulty: Beginner

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons of sugar
  • 2 teaspoons of baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 1 and 1/2 cups of milk
  • 2 tablespoons of melted butter or vegetable oil
  • A pinch of salt
  • A little oil for greasing the pan

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Add the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt to a mixing bowl.
  2. In another bowl, whisk together the egg, milk, and melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix until combined. Avoid overmixing the batter.
  4. Heat a lightly greased frying pan over medium-low heat.
  5. Pour a small amount of batter into the pan.
  6. Cook until bubbles appear on the surface and the edges begin to set.
  7. Flip the pancake and cook the other side until golden and cooked through.
  8. Repeat with the remaining batter and serve with your favorite toppings.

Serving Suggestions

  • Serve with honey or syrup.
  • Add sliced bananas or other fresh fruit.
  • Enjoy with a cup of tea, milk, or juice.
