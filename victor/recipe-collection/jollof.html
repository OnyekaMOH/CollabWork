About This Recipe

Nigerian Jollof rice is a popular rice dish prepared with tomatoes, peppers, onions, seasoning, and spices. It is enjoyed at family gatherings, parties, and everyday meals.

Recipe Information

  • Preparation time: 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: 45 minutes
  • Total time: 1 hour
  • Servings: 4 people
  • Difficulty: Intermediate

Ingredients

You will need the following ingredients:

  • 3 cups of long-grain parboiled rice
  • 4 medium tomatoes
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 1 medium onion
  • 2 tablespoons of tomato paste
  • 3 tablespoons of vegetable oil
  • 2 cups of chicken stock or water, plus extra as needed
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 teaspoon of curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon of thyme
  • Salt and seasoning to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Wash the rice thoroughly and set it aside.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, red bell peppers, onion, and garlic into a smooth mixture.
  3. Heat the vegetable oil in a pot and fry the tomato paste for a few minutes.
  4. Add the blended tomato mixture and cook until it thickens and some of the liquid evaporates.
  5. Add the curry powder, thyme, salt, seasoning, and chicken stock or water.
  6. Stir in the rice and ensure it is evenly coated with the tomato sauce.
  7. Cover the pot and cook over low heat until the rice is tender. Add small amounts of water if necessary.
  8. Stir gently, check the seasoning, and serve when the rice is fully cooked.

Cooking Tips

  • Cook the tomato mixture well to develop its flavor.
  • Use low heat to help prevent the rice from burning.
  • Avoid adding too much liquid at once.
  • Serve with chicken, fish, vegetables, or plantain.
