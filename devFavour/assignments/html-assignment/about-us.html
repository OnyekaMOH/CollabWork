Curious About the Web? So Are We!

The web has become such a familiar part of our lives that we rarely stop to wonder how it all works.

But behind every website, search, and click lies an interesting story.

Fun Facts About the Web is a small educational project created to explore those stories, share fascinating discoveries, and make learning about the web a little more exciting.

What We Do

We explore the technology, history, and often-overlooked details that make the web what it is today.

From understanding how browsers communicate with servers to discovering the origins of web technologies, our goal is simple: turn everyday curiosity into opportunities to learn something new.

You don't need to be an experienced developer to explore. Just bring your curiosity.
